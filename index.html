Manthan - Music Club

The official music club of IIT (ISM) Dhanbad was formed in the year 2016, is a vibrant community where students come together to create, perform, and grow through music. In Manthan, music brings us in, but it’s the bond that keeps us coming back. What truly sets Manthan apart is the bond we share—friendships forged over late-night jam sessions, shared stage nerves, and the pure joy of making music together. From classical to rock, indie to acoustic, it’s a space to explore your sound and find your voice.
